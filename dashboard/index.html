    How good is the model? Held-out validation

    Leave-one-out: each tokenized protocol's market cap is predicted from its TVL times the median multiple of its category, with the protocol itself excluded. A multiple model is a rough yardstick, not a precise predictor — that is why every row carries a wide uncertainty interval instead of a single confident number.

    Already has a token — reference only, never ranked

    These protocols record a token that already trades, so they are not launches. They are shown because their market caps calibrate the multiples above — nothing more.
